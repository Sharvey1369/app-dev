
# My Favorite Movie

## Coco (2017)

**Coco** is an animated movie about Miguel, a boy who loves music and dreams of becoming a musician. Along the way, he learns more about his family and their history.

### Why I Like It

- The story shows the importance of family.
- The music makes the movie enjoyable.
- The animation and colors are beautiful.
- It teaches us to understand our family's past.

### My Favorite Thing About Coco

*My favorite thing about Coco is how it shows that family is important, even when we have different dreams and opinions.*

The movie reminds us to appreciate our family and understand the people we care about.

> Family is important, and our memories help us stay connected.
